## Definition

The Project Format is one of the two alternating structures used by The Forge, LINQ's quarterly hackathon program. It is a dedicated four-day event where self-formed cross-functional teams build a working prototype and present it to judges. It is the format used when the business cycle allows a concentrated creative sprint — Q2 (before back-to-school crunch) and Q4 (to seed Q1 product roadmap).

Source: [`wiki/sources/forge-linq-hackathon-program.md`](../sources/forge-linq-hackathon-program.md).

## Teams

- **Self-formed.** Teams assemble organically around a problem; no pre-assignment by leadership.
- **Size.** Two to five people.
- **Cross-functional.** Open to all disciplines: engineering, product, design, support, and sales. Source code access is not required (contrast with the Race Format).

## Submission

- **Working prototype.** A functional artifact demonstrating the concept — not a slide deck or wireframe alone.
- **Demo.** A brief demo video or live presentation accompanying the prototype.

Both components are required for a complete submission.

## Judging criteria

Judges evaluate submissions on four criteria:

1. **Impact** — the potential business or operational value of the solution
2. **Creativity** — originality and novelty of the approach
3. **Feasibility** — realistic path to implementation or productization
4. **Theme alignment** — how well the submission addresses the season's stated theme

Specific judges and scoring rubric details are announced per season; they are not enumerated on the program-level page.

## Recognition and productization path

- **Project Format winners** present at the company all-hands.
- **Winning projects** are prioritized for productization — specifically, Season 4 (Customer Innovation) winners receive **dedicated Q1 sprint time** to build toward production.
- Prize details are announced per season.

## When The Forge uses this format

The Project Format is used in Q2 (Season 2 — Internal Efficiency) and Q4 (Season 4 — Customer Innovation) of the 2026 schedule. It is placed in quarters with capacity for multi-day focus: Q2 before the back-to-school crunch, and Q4 so prototypes can feed Q1 productization in time for spring sales cycles.

## Season 2 context (Q2 2026)

Season 2 is the current season (as of 2026-05-03). Its theme is **Internal Efficiency**: internal tooling, workflow automation, operational improvements, and AI-assisted processes. Season 2 is the flagship event for LINQ's AI Enablement Program. The LINQ Hackathon May 2026 repository (this codebase) is a Season 2 Project Format submission.
