## Definition

The Forge Season 2 ("Every Minute Matters") is LINQ's Q2 2026 internal hackathon — a four-day Project Format event running May 4–8, 2026 (Central Time). It is a sub-event of [The Forge — LINQ Hackathon Program](forge-linq-hackathon-program.md) and uses the [Project Format](../concepts/project-format.md). Its theme: find a workflow that is too slow, too manual, or too painful — for LINQ staff or for customers — and simplify it with AI.

Source: [`wiki/sources/forge-season-2-every-minute-matters.md`](../sources/forge-season-2-every-minute-matters.md).

Season 2's strategic framing bridges Season 1 and the impact on LINQ's end users:

- **Lede (from the Confluence page):** <escape>"Season 1 was about momentum — 360+ PRs against Platform Health. Season 2 is about leverage."</escape>
- **Theme statement:** <escape>"Find a workflow that's too slow, too manual, or too painful — for us or for our customers — and simplify it with AI. Fewer steps, fewer handoffs, faster outcomes."</escape>
- **Tagline:** <escape>"Every minute you save a district staff member is a minute back toward students."</escape>

Source: [`wiki/sources/forge-season-2-every-minute-matters.md`](../sources/forge-season-2-every-minute-matters.md).

### Judging criteria

Five criteria, each scored 1–10; 50 points maximum. Judging philosophy: <escape>"We're not scoring volume; we're scoring impact."</escape>

| # | Criterion | Guiding question |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem Worth Solving | Does this problem eat real time today? |
| 2 | Time Compression | How dramatically does the solution simplify the workflow? |
| 3 | AI Application | Is AI doing real work, or is it cosmetic? |
| 4 | Working Demo | Did they ship it or pitch it? |
| 5 | Creativity & "Wow" Factor | Would I pull someone over to see this? |

Note: the Season 2 rubric differs from the four-criterion rubric (impact, creativity, feasibility, theme alignment) described on the program-level page. The Season 2 page is authoritative for Season 2 scoring.

Source: [`wiki/sources/forge-season-2-every-minute-matters.md`](../sources/forge-season-2-every-minute-matters.md).

### Preparation guidance

Teams are encouraged (not required) to prepare a short problem statement, one-pager, or lightweight PRD before May 4. Teams that prepare are expected to have a meaningful advantage.

## Relationship to the LINQ Hackathon May 2026 repository

This repository is a Season 2 Project Format submission. The project — an internal AI workflow system acting as a force multiplier for LINQ employees — directly addresses the Season 2 theme: it simplifies workflows that are too manual, reduces handoffs, and makes AI tooling load-bearing (not decorative). The five judging criteria are the benchmark against which the project's demo should be evaluated.
