  The Forge Season 2 ("Every Minute Matters") is LINQ's Q2 2026 internal hackathon, running
  May 4–8, 2026. Author: Eric Wood, CTO space (Confluence page 732856331). The event follows
  the Project Format — self-formed cross-functional teams of 3–5 people, four days, working demo
  required. The theme: find a workflow that is too slow, too manual, or too painful — for LINQ
  staff or for customers — and simplify it with AI. AI tooling must be load-bearing, not decorative.

  Judging uses five criteria scored 1–10 (50 points maximum): Problem Worth Solving, Time
  Compression, AI Application, Working Demo, and Creativity & "Wow" Factor. Submissions and
  results are tracked at forge.labs.linq.com; event discussion lives in the #event-the-forge
  Slack channel. Teams are encouraged (not required) to prepare a problem statement or lightweight
  PRD before May 4. No prize structure, judges roster, or daily schedule are listed on this page.
