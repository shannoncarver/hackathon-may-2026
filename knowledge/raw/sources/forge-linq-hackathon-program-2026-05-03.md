  The Forge is LINQ's internal hackathon program, created to give teams protected time to innovate,
  experiment, and handle housekeeping work that rarely surfaces in a prioritized sprint backlog.
  The program runs on a quarterly cadence; each quarter is a Season. Author: Eric Wood, CTO space
  (Confluence page 419659784).

  Two formats alternate across seasons. The Race Format is a biweekly challenge series spanning a
  full quarter — four races, each running Thursday 8 am to Friday 8 am Central Time — with results
  tallied on a cumulative season leaderboard. The Project Format is a dedicated four-day event where
  cross-functional teams of two to five people self-form, pick a problem, build a working prototype,
  and submit a demo (video or live presentation) for judging on impact, creativity, feasibility, and
  theme alignment.

  The 2026 schedule assigns a format and theme to each season: Q1 (Season 1) — Platform Health,
  Race Format; Q2 (Season 2) — Internal Efficiency, Project Format; Q3 (Season 3) — Platform
  Health, Race Format; Q4 (Season 4) — Customer Innovation, Project Format. The schedule is
  deliberately aligned to LINQ's school-year business cycle: Q2 efficiency work pays off during
  the Q3 back-to-school crunch; Q4 prototypes feed Q1 productization ahead of spring sales.

  Season 2 (Q2 2026, Internal Efficiency) is the current season. It is tied to LINQ's AI
  Enablement Program as its flagship event for driving AI adoption inside the organization.
  Winning projects from Project Format seasons are prioritized for productization; Race Format
  champions receive Slack recognition and all-hands acknowledgment. Prize details are announced
  per season.
